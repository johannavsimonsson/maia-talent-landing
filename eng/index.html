FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What consultants usually ask

Whose brand does the client see?+

Yours. The portal is hosted at your address with your logo, and reports are sent out in your name. MAIA is not visible to the client.

Who owns the client relationship?+

You do. You acquire your clients, sign the contracts, and set your fees. We do not broker assignments and do not sell directly to your clients.

What is the measurement based on?+

Validated instruments following the international gold standard for work environment research, with benchmarks from thousands of organizations across 45 countries.

How much time does it take for the client's employees?+

Three questions a week, taking about 20 seconds. Short and frequent surveys provide readable insights over time without wearing out response willingness.

What is required of me to get started?+

Your logo and your colors, then you invite your first client. You choose how much you want to configure in settings. The client's manager adds employees once, after which the measurement is automated.

How are personal data handled?+

Processing complies with GDPR. Contact us for complete documentation regarding processing, storage, and data processing agreements.

How much does it cost?+

For you as a consultant, the portal is free — the client organization pays for its measurements. Book a demo and we'll go through the pricing model.
